Bring your WLED-powered LED strip to life with sliding on and off animations. Instead of an instant switch, the LEDs light up one by one across the strip, and go dark the same way. The animation is sent straight to WLED over your local network, so even long strips slide fast and keep the colour you have chosen.

Set the animation speed and direction from device settings or automate everything with Homey Flows. Works with any WLED-powered strip on your local network without any cloud connection.
